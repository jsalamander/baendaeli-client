How to get your Solibändeli

Donate. Take your bracelet. Done.

  1. Scan the QR code Point your camera app or QR code scanner at the QR code on the screen and open the link that appears.
  2. Pay a) With TWINT: Select a donation amount or enter your own amount (minimum CHF 25), then pay with TWINT on the payment page. b) With a voucher: Buy a voucher with cash at the information stand and redeem it. You do not need to choose an amount.
  3. Take the ball The screen shows “Dispensing”. The ball is dispensed automatically. Take it from the outlet below and open it.
  4. Tear off the receipt After payment, a payment confirmation page appears. Please keep it. Then tear the receipt off at the bottom of the printer, take the bracelet from the ball and return the empty ball.
If anything goes wrong, please contact the official information stand. Thank you for your donation! <3
